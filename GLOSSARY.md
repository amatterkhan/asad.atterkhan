# Asad.exe

Asad Atterkhan's personal site: a living pixel-art room on the homepage, with The Lab, Workbench and the rest of the site around it.

## The room

**Room**:
The animated pixel-art scene on the homepage, 384×216 room pixels, where Asad and Storm live.
_Avoid_: hero image, banner, scene

**Vignette**:
A small, mostly still crop of the room shown on other pages (About, The Lab, Workbench).
_Avoid_: room (when it means these crops)

**Divergence meter**:
The Nixie-tube clock under the bookshelf. It shows Mauritius time, or a worldline's divergence.
_Avoid_: clock, Nixie clock (that is only the name of the code that draws it)

**A and S books**:
The two hardcover spines on the shelf marked A and S. They are a private detail and never change.

## Worldlines

**Worldline**:
One of four versions of the room: Normal, Alpha, Beta or Steins Gate. It applies only on the homepage and resets on reload.
_Avoid_: theme, mode, world

**Divergence**:
The six-digit number that identifies a worldline on the meter (Alpha 0.571024, Beta 1.130238, Steins Gate 1.048596).

**Outfit override**:
An outfit that a worldline forces on Asad while it lasts, without changing the outfit he is set to wear. Only Steins Gate has one, the lab coat.
_Avoid_: costume, skin

## Asad

**Outfit**:
What Asad wears in the room, picked by the owner in the admin wardrobe.
_Avoid_: skin, costume, wardrobe (the wardrobe is the set of outfits)

**Thought**:
A one-liner Asad now and then says in a small speech bubble. Each one has a context: general, night, storm or steins-gate.
_Avoid_: quote, one-liner, message, tip

**Featured thought**:
The single Thought the owner pins, said first on each visit when its context holds.
_Avoid_: pinned quote, highlight

## Identity

**Motto**:
"Somewhere between cloud nine and layer seven." It is the site's description and footer line. It is never explained.
_Avoid_: tagline, slogan

**Page subtitle**:
The fixed line under a page's heading (Home, About, The Lab, 404). It never rotates.
_Avoid_: tagline, Thought
