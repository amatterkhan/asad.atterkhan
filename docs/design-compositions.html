ART-DIRECTION STUDY

The home scene is a mood and framing reference. Its soft generated texture needs deliberate pixel-level cleanup and original asset production before it can ship. A distinct Lab scene and cat sprite are still pending.

DESIGN INTENT

  • Professional message appears before decoration.
  • Modern body typography; pixel labels stay small.
  • Warm scene color balances the indigo base.
  • Cat stays beside content, never over controls.

MOBILE RULES

  • Single-column hero and reading flow.
  • Reduced scene crop with useful negative space.
  • TOC becomes in-flow; tables/code stay contained.
  • Compact navigation preserves touch targets.

REVIEW CHECKLIST

Review message hierarchy, scene placement and crop, reading width, card density, cat placement, and palette. Production pixel art remains paused until these compositions are approved.

Prototype copy and sample cards are layout examples, not new articles, accomplishments, or additional published claims.
